\chapter{LEARNING ANALYTICS}

\section{Educational Data Mining \& Activity Collection}

The Learning Analytics module continuously collects and logs comprehensive learning activities on the backend platform, establishing a robust data foundation for behavioral tracking and evaluation. The primary tracking parameters include:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Study Duration: Measuring exact active learning hours spent daily and weekly across various subjects and practice modules.
	\item Quiz Performance: Recording correct-to-incorrect ratios, score trajectories, and completion speeds during quizzes and mock examinations.
	\item Learning Consistency: Tracking login frequency, streak counts, and adherence to established daily study schedules.
	\item AI Interaction Metrics: Monitoring the frequency and context of interactions with the AI Tutor to identify recurring conceptual bottlenecks.
\end{itemize}

\section{Multi-Role Analytical Dashboards}

The processed analytics data is visualized through dedicated web and mobile interfaces tailored to three distinct system roles:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Student Dashboard: Displays real-time competency growth charts, study habits summaries, and the Examination Performance Prediction score to keep learners aware of their target score readiness.
	\item Parent Portal: Provides simplified developmental reports, progress summaries, and automated study alerts so parents can monitor examination readiness without requiring deep technical knowledge.
	\item Teacher \& Admin Portal: Offers high-level insights into class-wide performance trends, student engagement distributions, and question bank item difficulty metrics to support continuous content refinement.
\end{itemize}
