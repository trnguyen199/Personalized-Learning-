Motivation
In recent years, the Competency Assessment Exam has emerged as a highly popular method for university admission in Vietnam. Unlike traditional exams that rely heavily on rote memorization, the Competency Assessment Exam requires logical reasoning, reading comprehension, analytical skills, and the ability to solve real-world problems. However, when preparing for the exam, students differ significantly in terms of their knowledge base, learning pace, target scores, and available study time. Most current test-preparation websites and applications still employ a static learning model, offering the same fixed study path and content to all users. This approach has notable limitations; students often waste excessive time reviewing material they have already mastered while neglecting areas where their knowledge is weak and further practice is needed.

Recognizing these shortcomings, our team chose this topic to foster a smarter and more effective learning environment . The rapid advancement of Artificial Intelligence and educational data analytics presents a significant opportunity for us to realize a personalized learning model. By integrating AI, the system can continuously monitor and analyze each student's learning behaviors, performance results, and learning styles. Based on this data, the AI automatically adjusts study plans in real-time, ensuring that assignments and learning paths remain appropriately challenging and focused at every stage. Consequently, we decided to develop an "AI-integrated system for personalizing study and Competency Assessment exam preparation paths." This project is not merely a technological product for our coursework ; it offers a practical solution that enables each student to follow a tailored revision plan, study at a pace suited to their abilities, and achieve optimal results.
